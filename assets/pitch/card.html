the agentic economy needs a settlement layer

A citation
is a payment.

Creators set their own citation price. Researchers pay per question, not per month. The sources that actually grounded an answer get paid inside that same request.

01

Question
is funded

A researcher asks, and funds it with a small stablecoin budget.

02

Evidence
is gathered

Candidate sources are narrowed to the ones that plausibly answer it.

03

Validators
decide

Independent reasoning, reconciled by consensus, evidence checked at the source.

04

Creators
get paid

Payout bound to the receipt. Verification fails, nobody gets paid.

Attribution without payment is a screenshot.
Payment without verifiable attribution is a guess.
